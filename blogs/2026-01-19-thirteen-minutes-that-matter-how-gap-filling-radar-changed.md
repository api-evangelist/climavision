On May 16, 2025, a powerful supercell tore across southern Kentucky, spawning a devastating long-track tornado. As the storm approached Somerset, Climavision’s KY91 radar detected a tornado debris signature at 02:27 UTC. Thirteen minutes later, the nearest NEXRAD radar confirmed what KY91 had already seen.
