Grid reliability is one of the most important considerations for utilities worldwide. As we experience more frequent and severe weather events driven by climate change, the ability to manage and protect electrical grids becomes more critical. Utilities are increasingly turning to high-resolution weather forecasting to prepare for and mitigate disruptions caused by extreme weather.
