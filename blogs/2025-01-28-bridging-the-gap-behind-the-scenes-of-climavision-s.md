Installing one of our supplemental, X-band weather radars is no easy feat. Every installation is unique and requires a full team effort and collaboration on many fronts including manufacturing, siting, and, finally, construction. The United States’ National Weather Service operates the most comprehensive weather radar network in the world known as NEXRAD.
