In an industry as critical as electric utilities, operational efficiency and grid reliability are top priorities. Yet, one major factor can disrupt even the best-laid plans: unpredictable weather. From heatwaves and cold snaps to severe storms and high winds, these events can create unexpected spikes in demand, strain grid stability, and drive-up operational costs.
